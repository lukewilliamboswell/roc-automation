[#risk-and-control-reference]
= Risk and control reference

roc-automation covers shared tooling, workflow boundaries and evidence for
building, testing and distributing Roc packages and platforms. Product-specific
security design and project governance remain with maintainers. The
xref:overview.adoc#scope-and-design-principles[scope and design principles]
explain that boundary.

This reference is an index to contextual explanations. The linked chapter owns
the rationale, mechanism, verification and assumptions; this table does not add
another set of requirements or report implementation/adoption status. A control
may reduce a risk without eliminating it. Configuration, observed execution,
content identity, provenance and product correctness are different evidence.

== Find the relevant explanation

[cols="2,3,3",options="header"]
|===
| Concrete risk | Principle and owner chapters | Verification and limits to inspect

| Executed code crosses into publication authority
| Separate execution from authority. See xref:security-model.adoc#risk-execution-authority[execution boundaries], xref:actions.adoc#risk-action-boundaries[Action wrappers] and xref:settings.adoc#risk-settings-enforcement[effective settings].
| Wrong-event/source rejection, read-only jobs, inert handoffs and a real blocked PR. Read-only permission is not a same-job process sandbox.

| Storage or an upgrade substitutes executable bytes
| Admit identities explicitly; treat caches as storage. See xref:artifact-verification.adoc#risk-artifact-admission[artifact admission], xref:pure-package.adoc#risk-utility-provisioning[provisioning], xref:environment.adoc#risk-tooling-selection[tool selection] and xref:maintenance.adoc#risk-input-upgrades[upgrades].
| Hash and cache-corruption tests, reviewed locks and cold/warm execution. Identifying bytes does not establish that upstream code is benign.

| Tested source or assets differ from the delivered release
| Preserve identity across the operation. See xref:workflows.adoc#risk-release-evidence[release evidence], xref:examples.adoc#risk-example-identity[example archives] and xref:linker-input-releases.adoc#risk-linker-publication[native-input publication].
| Exact-source/run binding, archive tests, complete inventories, post-upload verification and recovery tests. Matching hashes alone do not prove a reproducible build.

| Source roles or native targets are confused
| Make the selected input and target explicit. See xref:repository.adoc#risk-source-boundaries[source boundaries] and xref:platforms.adoc#risk-native-targets[native targets].
| Header/binding checks and actual link/run tests for every claimed target. Static declarations and cross-compilation do not establish native behavior or FFI safety.

| Partial, stale or unrelated results appear successful
| Complete evidence is required for a pass. See xref:nightly-compatibility.adoc#risk-compatibility-verdicts[compatibility verdicts], xref:adopting.adoc#risk-adoption-evidence[adoption] and xref:project-practices.adoc#risk-evidence-claims[evidence claims].
| Missing-target, stale-attempt, spoofed-origin, launch-error and failed-publication scenarios. A static snapshot keeps its last colour when its publisher stops.

| A test hangs, hides diagnostics or substitutes smoke for deeper testing
| Bound execution and preserve its meaning. See xref:testing.adoc#risk-test-evidence[test evidence] and xref:optional-workflows.adoc#risk-campaign-separation[campaign separation].
| Success/failure streams, explicit skips, child cleanup, small smoke workloads and retained reproducers. Smoke results do not establish performance or sustained search coverage.

| Utility copies, companions or interfaces drift
| Share versioned contracts and complete runtimes. See xref:shared-utilities.adoc#risk-utility-contracts[utility contracts] and xref:policy.adoc#risk-policy-evidence[policy evidence].
| Exact distributed-artifact tests, native execution, invalid configuration and companion/version checks. A clean policy result covers only the selected rules.

| Composition records omit components or bind the wrong artifact
| Bind claims to subjects and keep uncertainty explicit. See xref:software-bill-of-materials.adoc#risk-component-evidence[component evidence].
| Schema/graph checks, wrong-subject fixtures and transitive/build-only queries. Consistent records do not prove discovery completeness or vulnerability exploitability.

| Documentation or an assessment overstates the checks
| Keep claims bounded and rationale close to the task. See xref:documentation.adoc#risk-documentation-claims[documentation claims] and xref:contributing.adoc#explain-the-risk-and-control-where-they-apply[maintaining explanations].
| Executable examples, rendered-content/link checks and review of operational evidence. A valid publication does not prove every technical assertion.
|===

== Use research without turning it into a framework checklist

Use primary sources to challenge the model's assumptions and investigate a
specific scenario. For workflow authority and untrusted checkout, start with
https://docs.github.com/en/actions/reference/security/secure-use[GitHub's secure-use guidance].
For input, build and distribution tampering, the
https://slsa.dev/spec/v1.2/threats-overview[SLSA supply-chain threat analysis]
separates source integrity, build integrity and availability. The
https://owasp.org/projects/top-10-cicd-security-risks[OWASP CI/CD risk research]
adds practical scenarios involving dependency abuse, pipeline execution,
credentials, artifact integrity and logging. The
https://www.cyber.gov.au/publication/secure-by-design-foundations[ACSC Secure by Design foundations]
support choosing defaults and continuing assurance over a product's lifecycle.

These sources inform decisions; citing one does not establish conformance or
justify an unrelated control. For reconstruction claims, use the
https://reproducible-builds.org/docs/definition/[reproducible-build definition]
and evidence of recreated bytes, rather than substituting release hashes or
provenance. Runtime and advisory evidence retain their separate purposes.

For further work, follow
xref:contributing.adoc#investigate-before-expanding-the-model[the investigation process]:
a scoped agent researches an actual failure scenario and produces an issue and
proposal. Review the evidence and alternatives before changing the model. Put
implementation gaps and unverified hypotheses in issues, and keep this index
linked to the selected practice rather than an accumulating backlog.
